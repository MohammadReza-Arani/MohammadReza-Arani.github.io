## Abstract
This paper presents the practical design and implementation of an integrated electric and communication infrastructure platform for an educational smart microgrid and electric machines laboratory at the University of Tehran. The platform integrates wireless XBee end-points with Python-based graphical user interfaces (PyQt5) running on Raspberry Pi controllers to monitor, acquire, and analyze real-time electrical machine parameters, power flows, and network states. The architecture provides students and researchers with hands-on experimentation in smart grid telemetry, SCADA systems, and distributed energy resource control.
